15 / 50 / 15 SQUARED OFF THE SIDES AT 90°

Right-angle area calculations

Every part — 15, 50, 15 — is measured square to the right side and chained one after the other, the last 15 ft stepped off the far end of A’s 65 ft line. The top and bottom edges lean, so every part overlaps past one edge (red: ground the parcel does not have) and leaves a gap at the other (white: ground nothing claims). Both grow the further a part reaches from the side, and both close only at 90°.
