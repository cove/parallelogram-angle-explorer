ASSESSOR SEGMENTS 14.07 / 50.72 / 15.21 STEPPED OFF ALONG THE NORTH BOUNDARY

North-edge segment calculations

Stepped off along the 80 ft north boundary, the Assessor's 14.07 / 50.72 / 15.21 still total 80 ft, so on their own they leave no gap. But they do not fall where the true 15 / 50 / 15 marks do: the east mark sits 0.21 ft west of true and the middle-to-west mark 0.93 ft west. The recorded middle span therefore reaches 0.93 ft into the true western 15 ft boundary. Because a foot stays a foot measured along the edge, that 0.93 ft overlap is the same at every slant angle.
